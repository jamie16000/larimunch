# Larimunch Brand Guide

> **Single source of truth** — Claude, please read this file at the start of any session that touches Larimunch design or content. It captures the brand identity so we stay consistent across all touchpoints (website, packaging, social, email).

---

## Brand essentials

- **Name:** Larimunch
- **Domain:** larimunch.com
- **Tagline:** real food · real laughter
- **Category:** Snacks & mini-meals for kids
- **Stage:** In product testing (early-stage, learning website fundamentals)
- **Owner:** Lara

## Mission (placeholder — Lara to refine)

Larimunch makes real food for real kids — wholesome, fun snacks and mini-meals that make mealtime joyful instead of stressful. We believe good food and laughter belong together.

## Brand voice

- **Warm and playful** — like a friend who loves to feed your kids well
- **Honest and unfussy** — no marketing-speak; we say what we mean
- **Encouraging, never preachy** — we don't shame parents or shame snacks
- **Sprinkled with whimsy** — a little sunshine in every word

## Logo

Four variations live in this `brand/` folder:

- **Logo1 - circle with subheadline.jpeg** — full lockup with tagline, circular frame. Use for social profile, packaging.
- **Logo2 - website.jpeg** — horizontal lockup. **This is the primary website header logo.**
- **Logo3 - no subheadline.jpeg** — coral circle, white wordmark, no tagline. Use for icons, favicons, small applications.
- **Logo4 - no subheadline green.jpeg** — green circle variant. Use as alternative on cream backgrounds.

The wordmark uses a **handwritten / casual script style**. Web equivalent: Google Font **"Caveat"** (closest free match) for the wordmark feel, with a clean sans like **"Nunito"** or **"DM Sans"** for body copy.

## Typography (web)

- **Display / wordmark feel:** [Caveat](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat) — handwritten, friendly, matches the logo
- **Body:** [Nunito](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito) — rounded, warm, very legible at all sizes
- **Fallback:** system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif

## Imagery & illustration style

- Bright, natural light — food photography that feels like a sunny morning kitchen
- Real kids being real kids (messy, laughing, curious — not staged)
- Hand-drawn touches: sun rays, little doodles, dotted lines (the logo's sun motif is a recurring graphic element)
- Avoid: stock-photo perfection, cartoonish characters, anything that feels "branded"

## What Larimunch is NOT

- Not a diet brand
- Not a "clean eating" lecture
- Not aspirational-influencer aesthetic
- Not corporate-blue, not neon-bright
